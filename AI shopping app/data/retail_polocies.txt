# Retail Pilot – FAQ & Customer Support Guide

This document contains frequently asked questions and policies for **Retail Pilot**. It is used for customer support and chatbot responses.

---

## PRICING

### Are prices the same online and in-store?

Yes, prices are generally the same on our website and in our physical stores. However, if there is any discrepancy, the price displayed on the product tag in-store will be considered final.

All prices are displayed in Euros (€).

---

## PRODUCT AVAILABILITY

### Will you restock sold-out items?

If an item is marked as "Sold Out", you can request a restock notification. If the item becomes available within 15 days, you will receive an email alert. If the item is permanently discontinued, it will be removed from the website.

### Are items from past seasons available?

Yes, selected items from past seasons may be available online. Availability varies by stock.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

### Will I receive the same product as shown in photos?

Size charts are available on each product page. Please review them carefully before placing an order.

All trousers follow standard length guidelines. For exact measurements, please consult the size guide.

Composition and care details are listed on each product page under “Composition & Care”.

## ACCOUNT & SECURITY

### How can I recover my password?

Click on “Forgot Password” on the login page. You will receive a reset link by email.

Password requirements:

* Minimum 8 characters
* At least one uppercase letter
* Must be alphanumeric

### How can I update my account information?

Log in to your account and update your email, password, and personal details under “Account Settings”.

### What if my account is disabled?

Accounts may be temporarily disabled after multiple failed login attempts. Please contact support for assistance.

## ORDER PROCESS

### How do I place an order?

1. Select category and products.
2. View product details.
3. Choose size and color.
4. Add to cart.
5. Proceed to checkout.
6. Enter shipping details.
7. Select payment method.
8. Confirm order.

### Do I need to register to place an order?

No. You may checkout as a guest. Creating an account is recommended for easier future purchases.

### Can I place an order by phone?

No. All orders must be placed online.

## SHIPPING & DELIVERY

### Where do you deliver?

We deliver across Germany to residential and business addresses. PO boxes are not supported.

### How long does delivery take?

* Standard delivery: 5–7 working days
* Sale periods: 7–10 working days

### How much is shipping?

Flat shipping fee: €5 per order within Germany.

### What happens if I’m not home?

The courier will attempt delivery twice. If unsuccessful, the package will be returned to our warehouse.

## ORDER MODIFICATIONS & CANCELLATION

### Can I cancel my order?

Yes, if the order has not yet been dispatched. Please contact support immediately.

### Can I modify my order?

Once an order is completed, items and delivery methods cannot be changed. You may cancel and reorder if processing has not started.

## RETURNS, EXCHANGES & REFUNDS

### General Policy

* All purchases are non-refundable.
* Exchanges are accepted within 14 days.
* Items must be unused and in original condition.
* Tags and packaging must be intact.

### Store Exchanges

* Exchange within 14 days with invoice.
* Items exchanged at current selling price.
* Sale items: size exchange only.

### Online Returns

You may:

#### (a) Exchange in Store

* Bring invoice and product.
* Fastest option.

#### (b) Return by Courier

* Contact support within 14 days.
* Free return shipping (if applicable).
* Replacement will be issued after inspection.

### Damaged or Incorrect Items

* Report within 7 days.
* Return shipping covered by Retail Pilot.
* Replacement or resolution provided.
